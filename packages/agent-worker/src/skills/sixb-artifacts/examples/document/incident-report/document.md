# Harbor Point outage, October 3

The west wing lost cooling for four days because a rooftop unit locked out on high pressure and its
replacement compressor was special-order. No equipment was damaged; tenants were moved to the east
wing for two days.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Approve the replacement of both rooftop units by October 31 to avoid a repeat this winter.

## What happened

| Time | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Oct 3, 13:51 | RTU-2 raised a high-pressure alarm and locked out |
| Oct 3, 15:20 | Technician on site: condenser coil 40% blocked, fan motor over its rated current |
| Oct 3, 17:05 | Coil cleaned and contactor replaced; the unit locked out again within an hour |
| Oct 4, 09:00 | Compressor failure confirmed; replacement ordered with a four-day lead time |
| Oct 7, 14:30 | Compressor installed; cooling restored |

## Why it took four days

The unit is past fifteen years in service, and parts for its model are no longer stocked by the
distributor[^parts]. Earlier failures this quarter were repaired with salvaged parts that are now
used up.

> [!NOTE]
> RTU-1, the unit's twin on the east wing, has failed twice this quarter with the same symptoms.
