1. What changes for Harbor & Pine

What we heard

The business runs through Dana: calls, texts and what she keeps in her head. There is no shared schedule, so the crew gets its plan from talking to her.

The aim is not more work, but the same work running smoothly without everything passing through one person.

What we saw in the books

Work ranges from large custom projects to recurring maintenance and snow contracts.

Many estimates stay marked pending, so QuickBooks can't tell which ones are still live.

What we propose

Keep what already works, walking the site and QuickBooks doing the books, and add one path every job follows:

Call → Site visit → Estimate → Won → Scheduled → In progress → Invoiced → Paid

The goal

The same business, running more smoothly, with work moving through the company without Dana carrying all of it.

2. Scope of work

Fieldwise builds an app on top of QuickBooks, for the office computer and the crew's phones. QuickBooks keeps the books; the app handles everything around each job.

What we'll deliver

  1. Every job in one place. From first call to paid, including annual contracts.
  2. Site visits to estimates. Photos and notes become a draft estimate you price and send through QuickBooks.
  3. One shared schedule. The crew knows where to go, and customers get updates.
  4. Extras and invoices. Approved extras carry onto the invoice, and payments show on the job.

Your part

  • QuickBooks Online admin access
  • Your estimate terms and conditions
  • A short check-in each week

Not included

Payroll, time tracking, and a customer login or portal.

3. Timeline

Delivery is about three weeks from kickoff, with a review as each part comes together.

  1. Week 1: Kickoff and QuickBooks

    Connect QuickBooks and bring in customers, estimates, invoices and payments.

  2. Weeks 1–2: Jobs and customers

    Build the Today page, the jobs board and customer pages, then review them with you.

  3. Week 2: Site visits and estimates

    Build site visit capture and drafted estimates, then try them on a real job.

  4. Weeks 2–3: Schedule, crew and invoices

    Build the calendar, the crew view, extras and invoices.

  5. Weeks 4–5: Fixes and small changes

    Fix what comes up as you use it on real jobs.
